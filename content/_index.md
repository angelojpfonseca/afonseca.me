I trained as a mechanical engineer in Brasília and came to Germany in 2015 on
an exchange, measuring things at a Fraunhofer institute in Berlin while
finishing my degree. Since 2018 I have worked at Daikin in Germany. The title
says pre-sales consultant; the work has shifted, year by year, toward data —
first Python and Power BI automation, then a digital process redesign, now
retrieval-augmented LLM tooling for quotation work.

Evenings and weekends belong to a small home lab that runs on one rule:
anything touching personal data stays on hardware I control. It hosts more
experiments than it strictly needs to — local language models, home
automation, the occasional robot.

This site is deliberately small. A few pages, no trackers, nothing that
watches you read. It gets updated when there is something worth writing down.
